# BMO Governance References

This repository contains governance risk and compliance reference material aligned with NIS2 and IEC 62443 for IT and OT environments.

The focus is on operating models accountability structures and control governance rather than certification or audit claims.

## Scope
- Governance and accountability models for IT and OT
- Risk ownership and decision structures
- NIS2 aligned governance and operating principles
- IEC 62443 lifecycle governance concepts
- Incident and escalation governance
- Policy structure and control mapping references

## Governance Approach
The material published here is based on the following principles.
- Clear ownership and responsibility
- Separation between governance and implementation
- Risk driven decision making
- Alignment with regulatory intent
- Practical applicability in real operational environments

## Framework Alignment
NIS2 is referenced as a regulatory governance baseline focusing on organizational measures accountability and operational readiness.

IEC 62443 is referenced as a framework for OT security governance including zoning conduits and lifecycle management.

No certification or compliance claims are made.

## Usage
All content is provided as reference material.
Organizations must adapt governance structures to their legal operational and regulatory context.

## Notes
No client specific information is included.
All examples are anonymized.
This repository does not provide legal advice or compliance certification.
